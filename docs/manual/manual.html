1Your role as Project Admin

Anlet replaces the Excel questionnaires of the TM Forum Autonomous Networks (AN) maturity assessment. Each participant answers online; scores, benchmarks and comments are collected automatically.

Work in Anlet is organized in projects. A project is one assessment round for a client, for example AN Maturity Assessment 2026. It contains one or more organizations (the client, e.g. a telco group). An organization has NatCos (national companies, also called OpCos), each with a country. Every participant belongs to one organization and picks their NatCo.

RoleWhat they do
Application AdminDetecon. Creates projects and organizations, adds and removes Project Admins, manages email settings. Sees all projects.
Project Admin (you)Runs one project: chooses its questionnaires, sets up NatCos, adds members, sends login emails, opens and closes the assessment, follows progress on the dashboard and exports results. Can answer questionnaires for testing — those answers never count.
ExecutiveA client manager (e.g. CTO). Sees the dashboard of their own organization and project, without names or emails of participants. Can also answer questionnaires — their answers count.
Normal UserA client engineer or expert. Answers questionnaires and sees their own results.
What you cannot do as Project Admin
  • Create or delete projects or organizations, or add an organization to a project.
  • Make someone a Project Admin or Application Admin.
  • Delete user accounts or move a user to another organization.
  • Change the email (SMTP) settings.
Ask your Application Admin for these. The full overview is in the appendix.

2Signing in and finding your way

Sign in

Open the Anlet address you received and sign in with your email and password. Email addresses are not case-sensitive.

  • Forgot your password? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page. The link in the email is valid for one hour.
  • Temporary password: if you signed in with a password someone gave you, Anlet asks you to choose your own first (at least 12 characters).
  • Locked: after 10 wrong passwords in a row, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.

Choose where to work

As a Project Admin you normally have more than one place to work: the project (as Project Admin) and your own organization (Detecon). After sign-in, Anlet asks which one. Choose the line that ends in — Project Admin.

You can switch at any time with Working as in the header. Everything you see and change applies to the project and organization shown there.

Projects with several organizations You have one "Working as" line per organization of the project. Members, open/close and the dashboard are per organization — switch to the other organization to manage it. The Excel import (section 4.3) is the exception: it can add members to every organization of the project in one go.

3Running an assessment: the checklist

Your Application Admin creates the project and its organization(s) and adds you as Project Admin. From there, the usual order is:

  1. Check the questionnaires. Manage project → Questionnaires. Keep only the High-Value Scenarios (HVS) the client should answer (4.1).
  2. Set up the NatCos. Manage project → OpCos. One per national company, with its country (4.2). You can also let the Excel import create them.
  3. Add the participants. Usually with the Excel import (4.3); single people with Create user (4.4). Fill in NatCo, Working domain and Designation in the file so participants can start straight away.
  4. Give them access. Send welcome emails (they set their own password) or forward the temporary passwords from the import results (4.3, 4.5).
  5. Test it yourself. Answer a questionnaire with Answer as user. Your answers are not counted; reset them afterwards (6).
  6. Follow progress. On the dashboard: who has submitted, who is still in progress or has not started (5). Remind people by email outside Anlet, or send a password reset email if someone is stuck.
  7. Close the assessment when the deadline has passed (4.6). Answers are then locked.
  8. Export. Download all (Excel) and Download PDF report on the dashboard (5).
An assessment is open from the start Every questionnaire of the project accepts answers as soon as people have access. Participants can change and re-submit their answers until you close it.

4The Manage project page

Click Manage project in the header. The page has one section per task; click a section title to fold it away. Anlet remembers which sections you folded.

4.1 Questionnaires

This section lists the questionnaires (HVS) that members of the project can answer — in every organization of the project. Click Edit questionnaires, tick the ones you need and click Save.

  • (R) marks questionnaires that are still in review at TM Forum. Their content may still change.
  • Core Network Fault Management & Stability is one item: the two Core questionnaires always go together, because they combine into one Core Domain score (50 % each).
  • At least one questionnaire must stay selected.
  • Removing a questionnaire stops new answers. Answers already given and their results stay available; selecting it again brings it back.

4.2 NatCos (OpCos)

Participants pick their NatCo in their profile, and the dashboard compares NatCos. Each NatCo has a name and a country.

  1. If the name is not in the drop-down yet, add it under NatCo Names for this organization → type it → Add.
  2. Choose the OpCo / NatCo name and the Country.
  3. Click Create OpCo. The list below (click OpCos (n)) shows all NatCos.
Small organizations An organization without NatCos is shown as a single row under its own name. With exactly one NatCo, members without a NatCo are counted under it.

4.3 Import members from Excel

The fastest way to add participants — also for every other organization of the project.

  1. Click Download Excel template. The file has the sheets Instructions, Members, NatCos (the ones that already exist) and Lists.
  2. On Members, fill in one row per person (see the table below). Cells with a list have a drop-down.
  3. Back in Anlet, decide whether to tick Send welcome email, then click Choose Excel file and pick your file. Anlet checks every row before anything is created.
  4. Read the results: each row is created, existing, membership added or an error with the reason. Download the results file — it holds the temporary passwords of new people who did not get a welcome email.

Good to know
  • Spelling of NatCo, Working domain and Designation is matched loosely (case, spaces, punctuation), so "demo telco germany" finds "Demo Telco Germany".
  • People who already have an account keep their password. For them, only empty profile fields are filled; existing values are kept (the result says so).
  • Importing the same file twice is safe — existing people and NatCos are reported as "existing".
  • A person belongs to exactly one organization. Someone who already belongs to another organization gets the error "Already belongs to …".

4.4 Create a single user

For one or two people. The person is added to the project and organization you are working on.

  1. Enter the Email.
  2. Either tick Send welcome email (the person gets a link to set their own password, valid 7 days), or click Generate for a one-time password that you forward yourself.
  3. Choose the Role: Normal User or Executive.
  4. Click Create user. A one-time password is shown once — copy it now. The person must change it at first sign-in.

An email that already exists is simply added to this project (password unchanged).

One table per organization (click the organization name to open it), one row per person and project. Use Search by email or the filters in the header row.

4.6 Opening and closing an assessment

In Questionnaire settings, choose the Assessment, check the status and click Close (lock all responses here) — or Reopen.

  • Open (default): participants can answer, change their answers and re-submit at any time. Re-submitting recalculates their score.
  • Closed: every response of this organization in this project is locked. Participants see their results but cannot change anything. Other projects and organizations are not affected.
  • Close each questionnaire separately, and — in a project with several organizations — for each organization (switch with Working as).

5The dashboard (Organization Overview)

Home opens the dashboard of the organization and project you are working on. Choose the questionnaire under Assessment; every block below is about that questionnaire. Averages only count submitted responses. As Project Admin you see participants' emails; Executives see the same dashboard without them (section 8).

Downloads

  • Download all (Excel) — one workbook with every table of the page (benchmark, respondents, answer matrix per Cognitive Activity, comments).
  • Download PDF report — a report with the tables, charts, the Cognitive Activity diagram and the comments per question. It uses the NatCo/Country filter of the diagram.
  • Each table also has its own Export to Excel.

NatCo benchmarking

One row per NatCo: number of respondents and submissions, the average final score (0–4), the average end-to-end (E2E) automation rate and the average score per sub-scenario (e.g. Equipment, Processing Error). Click a column header to sort; use the filters to narrow the rows. The chart follows the table. For Core, the table shows Fault Management, Stability and the combined Core Domain score side by side.

Cognitive Activity diagram

The average score per Cognitive Activity — Intent, Awareness, Analysis, Decision, Execution — with the overall average. Filter by NatCo or Country to compare.

Respondents

Everyone who can answer, with NatCo, country, working domain, designation, status (Not started, In progress, Submitted) and final score. Filter Status to find who still has to submit.

Answer distribution

All questions, grouped by Cognitive Activity, numbered Q1, Q2 … as participants see them. Click a question to open it: the question and options with their scores, the number of answers A/B/C/D per sub-scenario with a chart, and the comments on that question.

Comment Collection

All comments of this questionnaire in one table, with NatCo, country, designation, Cognitive Activity, question and sub-scenario. Filter and export it to collect the client's remarks.

How the score works, in short Each option has a score (shown next to it, e.g. A · 4). A question is answered once per sub-scenario. The sub-scenario score is the weighted average of its questions; the final score is the weighted average of the sub-scenarios, using the weights from the TM Forum questionnaire. Questions whose best option scores less than 4 are "compensated" as defined by TM Forum, so a top answer can still reach 4. Skipped questions are left out (not counted as 0). Key Effectiveness Indicators, where a questionnaire has them, get a separate score.

6Answering yourself: test answers

Use Answer as user to try a questionnaire exactly as participants see it — useful before a kick-off or to demonstrate it. The blue note confirms these are test answers: they never appear in respondents, averages, benchmarks or comments.

Click Reset my answers (on the questionnaire or its results page) to delete your test answers and start again. This only works while the assessment is open.

7What a Normal User sees

This chapter follows a participant from first sign-in to results, so you can explain it or answer their questions.

First sign-in

With a welcome email, the participant sets their password through the link instead. If you filled in NatCo, Working domain and Designation in the import, the profile step is skipped.

Choosing a questionnaire

The participant chooses the HVS (e.g. Fault Management), then the domain (e.g. RAN). Only the project's questionnaires appear.

Answering

At the top: a note that Executives see answers without name or email, the questionnaire's Guideline (click to open), the progress bar, and one button per question, grouped by Cognitive Activity. Groups turn green when complete, amber while something is still open.

Each question shows its Cognitive Activity, weight, a note and — where TM Forum provides one — a Guideline for this question.

The participant chooses an option for every sub-scenario (each with its weight). Every click is saved immediately; they can stop and continue later.

Not applicable? Leave the row on No answer and write a comment, ticking the sub-scenario it applies to. The unanswered rows are highlighted and pre-ticked. Such a question is skipped: left out of the score, not counted as 0.

A comment without tick on that sub-scenario does not cover it — "None of the sub-scenarios" is only for general remarks.

Comments are optional otherwise, but they are valuable: they appear in the Comment Collection.

Review and submit

After the last question comes Review before submitting. It lists every unanswered sub-scenario without a covering comment. Answer here opens the question right on the review page; Submit unlocks when nothing is left.

Results

After submitting, the participant sees their score (0–4), a Cognitive Activity diagram, the score per question and sub-scenario, the E2E automation checklist and their comments. Export to PDF saves it. While the assessment is open, Review / edit your answers lets them change and re-submit.

Core Domain For Core, participants answer Core Network Fault Management and Core Network Stability. Once both are submitted, their results show the combined Core Domain score (50 % each), with a link to whichever half is still missing.

8What an Executive sees

An Executive lands on the same dashboard as you (section 5) for their organization and project, including the downloads — with one difference: participants are anonymous.

  • Participation replaces the Respondents table: per NatCo the number of participants, submitted, in progress, not started and the average score. No individual names, statuses or scores.
  • In the answer distribution and the Comment Collection, people appear as Participant 1, 2, … with their NatCo, country and working domain. The same person keeps the same number within a questionnaire.
  • Participants are told this on every questionnaire.

Executives can also answer questionnaires with Answer as user; their answers count like everyone else's. They need a Working domain and Designation in their profile (the NatCo is optional for them).

9Questions and problems

SituationWhat to do
A participant did not get the welcome email.Check the address in Existing users. Ask them to look in spam. Otherwise User ▾ → Temporary password and forward it.
A participant forgot their password.They can use Forgot password? on the sign-in page, or you send User ▾ → Send password reset email.
"Too many attempts" / sign-in paused.Wait 15 minutes, then sign in with the correct password or reset it.
A participant cannot submit.The Review step lists the open points: every unanswered sub-scenario needs an answer or a comment ticked for that sub-scenario.
A participant picked the wrong NatCo.The participant opens the profile page (the Anlet address followed by /profile), picks the right NatCo and clicks Continue. Their answers then count for the new NatCo.
Someone answered by mistake in the wrong role.Change the role in Existing users; their answers stay.
I want to remove someone who already answered.Not possible from your side (to protect results). Deactivate them, or ask your Application Admin.
A questionnaire is missing for participants.Check Manage project → Questionnaires.
Someone needs to change answers after closing.Reopen the questionnaire (4.6), let them re-submit, close again.
The dashboard shows "—" or no averages.Nobody of that NatCo/questionnaire has submitted yet; only submitted responses count.
